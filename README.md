D.F.P. 003: Security by Environmental Integration
​Objective: Neutralize potential security risks (theft/targeting) by shifting from "hardened perimeter" defense to "invisible ecosystem" integration.
​Trigger: Design phase for any new infrastructure, wearable, or site improvement.
​Procedure:
​Aesthetic Camouflage: Design all components (coils, cores, wires) to blend into the natural or existing environment (camouflage skins, light blooms, organic layouts).
​Functional Presence: Maintain active management of the site. A "functioning" system is naturally less attractive to scavengers than a "dead" or abandoned one.
​Micro-Integration: Prioritize small-scale, integrated technical components (e.g., conductive fibers) over large, easily strippable industrial scrap.
​Digital Manifesting: Maintain a running digital record of all installations to establish active ownership and systematic oversight.
​Philosophy: You are not "hiding" a target; you are creating a habitat. Security is achieved through Sophisticated Utility, which is inherently less interesting to those looking for simple, quick-turnover scrap.# D.F.P.-003-Security-by-Environmental-Integration
